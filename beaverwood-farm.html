Meet the herd

Your campmates have names. And opinions.

Over 100 animals roam freely around the farm, day and night. Guests come back year after year to see their favorites.

Ginger & Heidi

The cows, including baby Heidi, who will happily trade affection for lettuce and carrots.

Peaches

The potbelly pig and official morning wake up call for anyone camped nearby.

Marge

The donkey. Calm, curious and a guest favorite every single season.

Cheerio

Widely considered the GOAT of goats. Known to cuddle, and to steal onions.

Chickens & ducks

Plenty of feathered company, plus water birds out on the lake.

Sheep

Woolly, gentle and usually grazing somewhere near the pasture.

Baby goats

Kids, lambs and calves through the seasons. Expect a lot of cute.

Good to know

Six things to read before you book.

A farm full of loose animals is a special kind of camping. Here's what makes for a happy stay.

01

Ages 16 and up only

No children under 16. It keeps the farm calm for both campers and animals.

02

Priced per person

$30 per person, per night. Not per site or vehicle. Groups up to 8.

03

Rigs under 20 ft

Larger RVs will struggle to get through our gate. Pop ups and vans fit fine.

04

Tents at your own risk

It's rare, but animals can damage tents and we can't reimburse for it. If that worries you, this may not be your spot.

05

Bring the right gear

Shoes you don't mind getting messy, ear plugs for the rooster and bug spray.

06

Near Bethel Woods

A favorite base for concerts at Bethel Woods. Ask your hosts about getting there.
